Image assets of the metal_pen_holder scene. All are derived from the LeRobot v3 dataset
makermods/pen_holder_bi_20260723_161553
(https://huggingface.co/datasets/makermods/pen_holder_bi_20260723_161553), revision
f9f3d9959464d123a164b1e4bc11d0f0e56ad97d, license Apache-2.0.

top_plate.jpg   640x480 background plate of the static top camera
                (videos/observation.images.top): per-pixel temporal median over the 704
                frames sampled every 37th frame from video files 000-002 (all 41 episodes).
                Arms, cup and pen move between those frames, so the median keeps only the
                static desk, riser shelf, wall and the tray / cables at the right edge.
                JPEG q92.
shelf_wood.jpg  54x400 -> 400x54 shelf board: the plate's shelf-top pixels (rows 214-268,
                columns 95-495), flipped and transposed so texel rows run along world y and
                columns along world x (the scene lays it once over the board's top). JPEG q92.
desk_wood.png   512x512 desk tile: a 256x256 near-nadir patch of the right wrist video
                (episode 0, frame 230, rows 10-266, columns 20-276; no gripper or pen pixels),
                de-shaded (divided by a 30 px Gaussian low-pass, rescaled to its mean) and
                mirror-tiled 2x2 so it repeats seamlessly; ~0.25 mm/texel, laid as a 0.13 m tile.

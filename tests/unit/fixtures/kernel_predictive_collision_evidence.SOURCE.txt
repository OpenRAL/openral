Scenario: the PREDICTIVE counterpart of
`kernel_reactive_collision_evidence.json`. A planar 2R arm seeded at
q = [0, pi/2] (end effector clear of a voxel wall at y >= 1.3) receives a
CARTESIAN_DELTA chunk of ten +0.05 m steps along +y. The measured
configuration passes the reactive check; step 5 of the kernel's own
damped-least-squares Jacobian integration does not.

`joint_positions_rad` is that step-5 configuration — [0.29982877677088093,
1.2524345104800854], nowhere near the measured seed. It is the field this
fixture exists to pin: the configuration a predictive verdict is *about*
exists in no other artifact (it depends on the kernel's lambda and its seed
dt), so before it was recorded, a predictive stop could only be adjudicated
against the measured joints — a different pose, and in the drawer-opening run
that motivated the change, one where the two links the kernel reported at
-5.34 mm sit +53 mm apart.

Note the precision: every number is serialized at `max_digits10`, so the
payload round-trips to the identical doubles. The default 6 significant digits
would have rounded these joint angles to ~1e-6 rad, which at a metre of reach
is millimetres of end-effector error — the same order as the distances the
evidence exists to adjudicate.

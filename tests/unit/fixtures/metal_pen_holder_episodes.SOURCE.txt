                           columns (left joint1..6 in degrees with the URDF sign / zero, left
                           gripper stroke ~0 closed .. ~105 open, then the right arm), 30 fps.
  layout_<ep>              float32 (6,): the episode's start layout in station_base (the
                           midpoint of the arm bases): cup x, y (m), cup face yaw (deg, mod 90),
                           pen centre x, y (m), pen yaw (deg). Cup from left-arm forward
                           kinematics at the grasp (0.087 m along gripper_base z); pen from
                           top-camera back-projection with the measured station camera.

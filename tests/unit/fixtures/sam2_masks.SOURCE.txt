The second mask is the load-bearing regression fixture for the whole vision
attachment-evidence design: it proves the segmenter's own score cannot detect a
mis-aimed prompt, which is why every fail-closed gate in
openral_hal._vision_attachment_evidence is geometric and never the mask score.

Both source frames are in-tree rSkill media; both are captures of OpenRAL's own
